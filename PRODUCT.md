# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Film and television enthusiasts who want a dedicated personal tracking system to log watch history, organize what they plan to watch, analyze viewing habits over time, and discover personalized recommendations.

## Product Purpose

Memento provides an intimate, ad-free personal cinema journal and recommendation companion. It empowers users to record what they watch across film and television, view detailed insights and viewing analytics, manage watchlists, and find quality recommendations without commercial noise or distraction.

## Positioning

A lightweight, distraction-free personal tracking journal powered by TMDB, OMDb ratings, and YouTube trailers, emphasizing personal viewing history, analytics, and private curation over social feeds or algorithmic bloat.

## Operating Context

- Browsed on desktop and mobile web viewports.
- Integrated with TMDB API for catalog metadata, OMDb for IMDb rating context, and YouTube for official trailers.
- Multi-page client-side architecture (`index.html`, `pages/*`) with vanilla JavaScript (`assets/app.js`) and modern CSS (`assets/styles.css`), backed by a lightweight Node proxy server (`backend/server.js`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Multi-category catalog**: Movies, TV Shows, Anime, Documentaries, Someday / Watchlist, History, and Analysis views.
- **Watch History & Analytics**: Log viewings, view timeline history, and explore viewing statistics.
- **Rich Media Details**: Detailed title cards, expanded plots with spoiler safeguards, IMDb and TMDB ratings, and embedded official trailers.
- **Privacy & Simplicity**: No mandatory social features, tracking bloat, or third-party ads.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: Memento ("Your watch history").
- **Aesthetic Tone**: Cinematic dark theme (`#050405`), restrained typography, focused media presentation.

## Product Principles

1. **Viewer-First Simplicity**: Keep interactions direct, responsive, and uncluttered by marketing noise or forced social interaction.
2. **Cinematic Respect**: Showcase poster artwork, film details, and trailers with high visual craft and respectful framing.
3. **Actionable History & Discovery**: Make logging effortless and transform past viewing history into genuinely helpful discovery and meaningful personal analysis.
4. **Data Integrity & Privacy**: Treat user watch lists and viewing habits as private, durable personal records.
